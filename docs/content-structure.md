# Shivani Arya — Content Structure

## Core Experience

The website is a cinematic portfolio for Shivani Arya.

Primary disciplines:

- Acting
- Modelling
- Fashion
- Digital Creation

The website should feel like a creative journey rather than a conventional résumé.

---

# 1. Opening Experience

## Loading / Intro

Purpose:

Create anticipation before entering the website.

Possible elements:

- Shivani Arya name
- Minimal loading indicator
- Subtle film grain
- Typography reveal
- Short cinematic transition

The intro must be skippable or very short.

It must not delay access to the actual website unnecessarily.

---

# 2. Hero

## Purpose

Immediately establish Shivani's identity and visual personality.

Content:

SHIVANI ARYA

ACTRESS · MODEL · CREATOR

Primary visual:

Hero portrait or cinematic image.

Core statement:

A WOMAN IN MANY FRAMES

Possible supporting interaction:

ENTER / EXPLORE

---

# 3. Introduction

## Purpose

Introduce Shivani as a person and creative professional.

Content:

- Short biography
- Location
- Creative disciplines
- Personal statement
- Portrait

The biography should remain concise.

The visual design should communicate more personality than a long block of text.

# 5. Selected Work

## Purpose

Show professional and creative work.

Potential categories:

- Acting
- Modelling
- Fashion
- Digital Creation
- Photography

Each project may contain:

- Project title
- Year
- Category
- Role
- Images
- Video
- Description
- Credits
- External link where relevant

Only available information should be displayed.

Do not invent credits or projects.

---

# 6. Showreel

## Purpose

Give visitors an immediate way to experience Shivani's performance.

Primary content:

Showreel video.

Interface:

PLAY REEL

The video experience should support:

- Desktop
- Tablet
- Mobile

Video should not autoplay with sound.

---

# Information Priority

2. Her visual presence
3. Her professional disciplines
4. Showreel

6. Contact

Secondary:

- Biography
- Archive
- Additional information

---

# Content Rules

Do not fabricate:

- acting credits
- brands
- productions
- awards
- directors
- professional relationships
- measurements
- education
- achievements

Placeholder content should be clearly marked during development.

Real information will be supplied by Shivani.

---

# Future Expansion

The structure should allow future additions such as:

- New acting projects
- New campaigns
- New photoshoots
- New showreels
- Brand collaborations
- Press appearances
- Awards
- Testimonials
- Booking/management information

The architecture should allow new projects to be added without redesigning the entire website.
